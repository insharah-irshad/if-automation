# IF Automation

AI automation systems built to turn repetitive business processes into intelligent workflows.

IF Automation is an AI automation studio focused on building practical automation systems, AI agents, and workflow-driven solutions for modern businesses.

---

## About

Businesses spend a lot of time handling repetitive tasks across emails, forms, CRMs, spreadsheets, messaging platforms, and internal tools.

IF Automation focuses on connecting these systems with AI and automation so repetitive work can be handled by software while people focus on decisions, creativity, and growth.

The core idea is simple:

**AI + Automation + Business Systems**

---

## What We Build

### AI Agents

Intelligent systems that can understand tasks, make decisions, use tools, and interact with business workflows.

### Workflow Automation

Automated workflows connecting applications, APIs, databases, webhooks, and AI models.

### Business Process Automation

Systems designed to reduce repetitive manual work and streamline everyday operations.

### AI-Powered Solutions

Custom AI systems built around specific business problems instead of generic chatbot experiences.
